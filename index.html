Kevin P. Gaffney

Applied Scientist at Microsoft Gray Systems Lab

About

I'm an Applied Scientist at Microsoft Gray Systems Lab in Barcelona, where I work on database systems research. I received my PhD in Computer Sciences from the University of Wisconsin–Madison in 2024, advised by Jignesh M. Patel in the Database Systems Group. Previously, I earned Bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and Biochemistry from the University of Oklahoma in 2018.

My research focuses on maximizing the performance of database systems by leveraging modern and emerging hardware.
